Compass Minerals Comments on Salt Exclusion from Section 338 Tariffs

OVERLAND PARK, Kan.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Compass Minerals International, Inc. (NYSE: CMP) announced that effective September 15, 2026, salt and pure sodium chloride produced in Canada are excluded from the 50% ad valorem Section 338 tariffs. The company operates the world's largest underground salt mine in Goderich, Ontario, serving communities throughout the Northeast and Midwest by helping keep roads, bridges and other transportation infrastructure safe and operational during snow and ice events.

"We are grateful the Trump Administration recognized the critical role our salt products play in public safety and interstate commerce. We would also like to thank U.S. Senator Jerry Moran and others who worked alongside him for their advocacy on behalf of Compass Minerals and our industry."

About Compass Minerals

Compass Minerals (NYSE: CMP) is a leading global provider of essential minerals focused on safely delivering where and when it matters to help solve nature's challenges for customers and communities. The company's salt products help keep roadways safe during winter weather and are used in numerous other consumer, industrial, chemical and agricultural applications. Its plant nutrition products help improve the quality and yield of crops while supporting sustainable agriculture. Compass Minerals operates 11 production and packaging facilities with more than 1,800 employees throughout the U.S., Canada and the U.K. Visit compassminerals.com for more information about the company and its products.
