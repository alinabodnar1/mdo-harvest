Tonly showcases DTE95 battery electric mining truck at Steinexpo

In recent years, Xi'an-based Tonly Heavy Industries has continued its globalisation development strategy. This includes Europe, and at Steinexpo 2026 at the MHI basalt quarry in Homberg, Germany, one of the leading events in the European quarrying and mining industry calendar, it had its 50 t class DTE95 battery electric wide-body dump truck on show.

As early as 2020, Tonly's all electric mining vehicles began large-scale implementation in mining; and it argues that it went on to successfully set a benchmark for the application of battery electric equipment in the industry, providing a replicable and scalable leading example for the mining's green transformation. Today, Tonly's mining BEV's have spread all over the world's major open-pit mining areas, and have been put into operation at mines in many countries including Peru, Brazil, Guinea and Serbia.

It says the DTE95 pure electric wide-body dump truck is a new energy product created by Tonly for the low-carbon transformation of global mines and benchmarked against the international high-end market. It adds that it is also the industry's first electric-only wide-body dump truck with wide-base tyres. Tonly: "The product combines the advantages of zero emissions, low energy consumption, high reliability and strong adaptability to accurately match the development trend of green production and low carbon operation."

Tonly adds that the wide-base tyre structure design effectively reduces the specific ground pressure and improves traction in complex and harsh working conditions such as soft ground, mud and poor haul roads in mining areas, and such has outstanding environmental adaptability. The truck at the event was fitted with tyres from Aeolus.

At the same time, the vehicle chassis has ample space for intelligent upgrades, which can be adapted to multiple driverless systems, support intelligent fleet formation and unmanned dispatching operations, and take into account the needs of efficient production and smart mine upgrades, providing green, intelligent and efficient transportation for mines in Europe and globally.

Currently in Europe, the DTE95 pure electric wide-body dump truck has already arrived in the Polish market where it is being tested by ŚGP INDUSTRI at its Jaźwica dolomite mine. Tonly adds that the stability and reliability of the product have already been initially verified in other overseas markets. Tonly says it will continue to iteratively upgrade its new energy mining equipment and overall solutions, accurately matching the working condition characteristics and standard requirements of its trucks to different countries and different types of mining areas.
