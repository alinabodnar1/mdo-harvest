Normet opens technology centre to accelerate the future of underground mining

Normet has celebrated the grand opening of its new Technology Center in Pirkkala, Finland, a facility that, it says, strengthens the company's capabilities in research, development and testing of advanced technologies that improve the safety, productivity and sustainability of underground operations.

The new centre will serve as a hub for autonomy, robotics, software development and other digital technologies that are shaping the future of mining, Normet says. As mines become deeper and operating conditions more demanding, technologies that enable remote and autonomous operations play an increasingly important role in moving people away from hazardous environments.

Ed Santamaria, President and CEO of Normet, said: "Today is an important milestone for Normet. This investment expands our technology development and testing capabilities and reinforces our commitment to staying at the forefront of innovation in underground mining. Customer demand for advanced mining technologies continues to grow, and this centre will help us accelerate the development of solutions that improve safety, productivity and sustainability underground."

Normet generates more than 95% of its revenue outside Finland and serves customers through approximately 50 locations across 30 countries. The company manufactures mining equipment in Finland, Chile and India and is experiencing strong global demand, supported by growth in underground mining driven by the green transition, urbanisation and increased investment in critical minerals, it says.

The Technology Center will also provide a dedicated environment for customer demonstrations, showcasing teleremote operation, autonomous functions and battery-electric equipment.

Pirkkala was selected for its proximity to one of the world's leading ecosystems for electrified mobile work machines and mining technology, strong research networks, Tampere University and a highly skilled technology talent pool, the company explains. Its location also offers convenient access for international customer and partner visits.

Santamaria concluded: "This investment continues our long-term commitment to Finland and our technology leadership. We have already hired more than 50 people in Finland this year to support our growth, particularly in manufacturing, and we expect this new centre to create additional high-value technology roles in the future."
