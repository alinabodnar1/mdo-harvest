Voith launches PowerDrive Mining electrified conveyor drive system

Voith is launching PowerDrive Mining, its new electric drive system for belt conveyors, at Electra Mining Africa 2026 in Johannesburg. The company is presenting the solution at the Johannesburg Expo Centre, Nasrec, from September 7 to 11, 2026.

With PowerDrive Mining, Voith says it offers a "cost‑attractive, fully coordinated conveyor drive and control solution that can be installed fast and operates reliably, efficiently and sustainably while reducing interface risk." Voith ships the package pre-assembled and pre-programmed, pairing compact high-power hardware with coordinated multi-drive control to support faster commissioning, smoother starts and confident daily operation.

PowerDrive Mining is designed as a compact, electrified drive package for belt conveyor applications. It combines key drivetrain elements and safety-relevant components into one coordinated system and supports customers with engineering and services from a single source. This approach Voith says reduces coordination effort and lowers integration risk in conveyor projects that typically involve several suppliers and interfaces.

A core element of the solution is BeltControl – a controller specifically designed for the requirements of belt conveyor systems. Its integrated control logic supports smooth start-ups, precise acceleration and braking, synchronised multi-drive operation, and reduced mechanical stress on belts and steel structures. This helps operators maintain stable operation of the belt conveyor application and reduce wear in demanding mining environments.

Mining conveyor projects often face tight schedules and limited shutdown windows. PowerDrive Mining addresses this by reducing work on site. Voith delivers preassembled and factory-tested hardware and provides a pre-parameterised controller concept. Teams do not need to implement project-specific programming; they can work with parameterisation instead.

This reduces engineering effort, lowers error potential, and shortens quotation, engineering and commissioning time. Its compact form factor improves feasibility for retrofits and replacements in tight conveyor galleries and constrained layouts.

PowerDrive Mining is developed for strong cost-efficiency and high sustainability. The system targets "attractive investment cost and price-performance ratio through an integrated scope of supply, material-optimised design and reduced coordination effort, with purchase price levels around 30% below the market benchmark."

Overall, PowerDrive Mining is designed as an integrated conveyor drive system that combines competitive investment cost with fast, low‑risk installation, reliable operation and clear system responsibility throughout the project lifecycle.

For operators, uptime and safe operation matter most. The PowerDrive Mining concept addresses this with a fully integrated drive package, coordinated multi-drive control, and design features engineered for high durability and long service life under harsh mining conditions. It also supports faster troubleshooting and lower service costs through remote diagnostics, clear error messages and a globally standardised operating concept. Safety is engineered right into the system: The concept includes an integrated brake and backstop, enclosed rotating components and a robust housing design.
