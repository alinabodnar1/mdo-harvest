Master Drilling awarded shaft raiseboring contract by Boliden Somincor

Master Drilling has secured its inaugural Portugal contract with Boliden Somincor, a copper and zinc mining company, to construct two raisebored shafts measuring 630 metres deep and 4.5 metres in diameter.

The company achieved a significant operational milestone by completing the initial pilot hole in 24 days using a single pilot bit throughout the operation. This accomplishment benefited from the Micon RVDS guidance system, which Master Drilling describes as enabling "exceptional accuracy."

Master Drilling expressed appreciation for "Boliden Somincor for their confidence in Master Drilling, and to our valued partners Epiroc and Micon Drilling for their contribution."

The Micon RVDS functions as a pre-programmable, self-steering mechanism for vertical drilling. Its design incorporates two modules positioned in the lower section of the bottom hole assembly between the drill bit and the primary string stabiliser, complemented by a surface read-out unit containing a pressure transducer, interface unit, and driller cabin computer.
