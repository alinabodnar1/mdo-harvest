Barloworld signs distribution deal with Hermann Paus Maschinenfabrik

Today, at Electra Mining Africa 2026 in Nasrec, Johannesburg, Barloworld signed a new distribution agreement with Hermann Paus Maschinenfabrik GmbH, bringing the German manufacturer's underground mining vehicle range into its offering across Barloworld's operating countries which include South Africa, Swaziland, Botswana, Lesotho, Malawi, Mozambique, Zambia, Angola and Zimbabwe.

PAUS builds specialised vehicles for underground mining and tunnelling. Paired with Barloworld's parts, service and support network across southern Africa, it means faster access to purpose-built underground equipment, backed by the same aftermarket commitment Barloworld customers already rely on above ground. The agreement was signed on-site by Barloworld CEO Dominic Sewela and Franz-Josef Paus, Managing Director of Hermann Paus Maschinenfabrik.

Sewela stated: "For me, this partnership is ultimately about one thing: serving our customers better. Underground mining is a demanding environment, and our customers increasingly need more than individual pieces of equipment. They need partners who understand their operations and can bring together the right equipment, technology, technical expertise and lifecycle support."

He added: "By combining our Cat underground production equipment with PAUS's specialised utility and support solutions, we are strengthening our ability to offer customers a more comprehensive underground mining solution across southern Africa."

PAUS is based in Emsbüren and produces a diverse range of specialised vehicles for underground mining operations, including but not limited to trucks for material handling, fuel & lube transport plus platform and crane trucks & multi purpose tool carriers as well as narrow vein LHDs and dump trucks. It also supplies rock breakers, water cannons, roadheaders, personnel carriers, tyre handlers, graders, scalers, rail vehicles, explosive chargers and transporters, concrete sprayers and mixers, fire fighting trucks and mine rescue trucks.
