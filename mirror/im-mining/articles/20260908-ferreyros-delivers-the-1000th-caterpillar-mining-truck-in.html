Ferreyros delivers the 1,000th Caterpillar mining truck in Peru

Ferreyros, a leader in heavy machinery, has delivered to Compañía Minera Antamina the 1,000th Caterpillar mining truck in Peru, which it described as a historic event in the machinery industry. The truck, a 410 short ton (372 t) payload Cat 798, was incorporated into the operations of the Antamina copper-zinc mine at 4,300 m above sea level, after a commemorative ceremony in the presence of leaders from both companies and Caterpillar.

Since the introduction in 1994 of the first Caterpillar truck – a 100-ton CAT 777 at Cerro Verde – the portfolio has undergone a permanent evolution in key dimensions, such as payload and cutting-edge technologies. Highlights include the adoption of Peru's first giant trucks, with 400 short ton Cat 797 units in 2011, and the pioneering implementation of Cat 794 autonomous mining trucks, five years ago, with Ferreyros' 24/7 support.

For more than 30 years, Caterpillar trucks have been part of the history and high production objectives of world-class Peruvian mining operations such as Antamina, Antapaccay, Cerro Verde, Constancia, Cuajone, Las Bambas, Mina Justa, Pierina, Quellaveco, Toquepala, Toromocho and Yanacocha, which are characterised by their permanent contribution to the development of Peru.

"Ferreyros is honored to celebrate the legacy of 1,000 Caterpillar trucks in our country. This milestone reflects the expansion of the mining sector and its development possibilities; the trust placed by our customers in Ferreyros and Caterpillar; and the strength of our long-term ties," said Gonzalo Díaz Pró, General Manager of the century-old company.

"The delivery of the 1,000th Cat truck reaffirms Ferreyros and Caterpillar's leading contribution to Peruvian mining and our vision for the future. We will continue to boost the productivity and reliability of Cat fleets, with dedicated support, and we project new horizons, such as electrification and the expansion of innovative technologies, hand in hand with our customers," said Santiago Basualdo, Central Manager of Ferreyros Large Mining.

This event occurs in a year of significant mining operations. Recently, Antamina expanded its fleet of ultraclass Cat 798 trucks to reach 45 units. Likewise, Minera Las Bambas awarded Ferreyros the tender for a large fleet of machinery for large-scale mining, with the possibility of extending to 45 units – the entire fleet of ultraclass trucks, made up of 27 Cat 798s, stands out.

Ferreyros' 24/7 support at mines it addds is backed by industry-leading capabilities, with continuous investments; commitment to the safety and productivity of operations; and a transformative vision for mining services.

It highlights the company's national network of workshops, which includes component repair centres in Lima and La Joya (Arequipa), in addition to an electrical components workshop in the latter complex. These are certified by Caterpillar as world-class facilities. Ferreyros also has a robotic spare parts distribution centre; warehouses in the mines themselves, monitoring centres and laboratories; and a team committed to world-class service.
