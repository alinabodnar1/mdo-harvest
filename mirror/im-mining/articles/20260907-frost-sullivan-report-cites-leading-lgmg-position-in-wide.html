Frost & Sullivan report cites leading LGMG position in wide body trucks

Frost & Sullivan, the global consulting firm, recently completed a specialised market research report, which named Jinan-based LGMG as having the number one position globally by annual sales volumes of off-highway wide-body dump trucks for 10 consecutive years between 2016-2025.

LGMG says that Frost & Sullivan conducted multidimensional, long-term data tracking across the global rigid dump truck market, "covering core metrics such as sales data from major mining regions and engineering markets worldwide, end-user feedback, and manufacturers' production capacity and overseas deployment."

Other major wide body truck suppliers include LiuGong, SANY, XCMG, Weichai LOVOL, Zoomlion, Sinotruk, Yutong and others including Xi'an based Tonly Heavy Industries, which originally introduced the wide body truck to the market back in 2004 and also claims the the top spot in terms of sales.

LGMG stated: "A decade of global leadership reflects the long-standing trust that thousands of mining customers worldwide have placed in LGMG's product strength and quality reliability. Built on superior reliability, high uptime performance, full-scenario adaptability, and a global localised service network, LGMG's wide-body mining trucks serve large-scale open-pit mines, aggregates, and earthmoving projects both at home and abroad. They consistently deliver stable, high-efficiency heavy-load transportation solutions, withstanding diverse complex operating conditions and extreme environments, and solidifying their global leadership through proven product performance and service excellence."

LGMG says behind its success is its founding brand promise of reliability in action. "From R&D and design to manufacturing, from quality control to after-sales service, LGMG has embedded reliability throughout the entire value chain. With steadfast dedication to the mining transport sector, the company has charted a path of global advancement for Chinese high-end equipment, driven by consistent product performance and exceptional service assurance."

Looking ahead, LGMG says it will continue to deepen its presence in the mining equipment sector, accelerating technological innovation and product upgrades. "Leveraging reliable products, global localised services, and an integrated supply chain system, the company remains committed to strengthening its global competitive edge and setting the benchmark for Chinese high-end equipment on the world stage."

LGMG CEO Simon Zhang at a special press conference on August 28 emphasised that this achievement belongs to customers, partners, and all LGMG employees, and that reaching the top is not an endpoint but the starting point of greater responsibility. He also stated that its status stems from the trust of global customers who entrust LGMG with their production tasks under extreme working conditions.

Wang Yinshu, Head of R&D Center at LGMG, said that leveraging its global R&D footprint, LGMG has mastered full-stack core algorithms in electrification, with industry-leading thermal management and battery life technologies. He added that in terms of intelligence, the company has achieved independent breakthroughs in full-link chassis-by-wire technologies and developed a proprietary telematics system that drives mining operations toward unmanned and highly efficient management. "Based on modular platforms and scenario-based customisation strategies, LGMG continues to launch diversified powertrain products and has proactively laid out integrated solutions for solar-storage-charging microgrids and smart mines, committed to advancing from digital management to AI-powered intelligent dispatch, driving global market expansion through technological innovation."

Zhang Likuan, Vice President of the China Mechanical and Electrical Products Circulation Association (CMEPCA), pointed out that to comprehensively promote the green development of the mining industry, three key tasks will be advanced in tandem: first, conducting regular surveys and assessments of mineral resource development and utilisation to strengthen the most efficient use of resources; second, promoting the adoption of advanced and applicable technologies to inject innovation-driven momentum into mining quality and efficiency improvement; and third, deepening the construction of green mines to achieve deep integration of mining development and ecological protection. He stressed that these three tasks support each other and form a closed loop, steadily advancing the green and high-quality development of the mining industry. He went on to fully acknowledge LGMG's contributions in promoting the green and intelligent upgrading of mining equipment.

In response to the trends of green development, new energy, and intelligent transformation in the mining industry, LGMG says it will refine its products based on real-world operating conditions of global customers, accelerate the implementation of new energy technologies, improve its global service network, and remain reliability-focused to continuously create value for the global mining industry.
