BHP awards Worley contracts for Copper South Australia projects

Worley has been awarded contracts by BHP to provide study and engineering, procurement and construction management (EPCM) services for expansion projects at Olympic Dam and Carrapateena, which remain subject to final investment decision by BHP.

The projects form part of BHP's Copper South Australia growth plans to increase production as demand continues to grow, driven by electrification and the energy transition.

Worley will deliver study and EPCM services concurrently across multiple project phases for potential block cave development and associated infrastructure at Olympic Dam, and the potential expansion of Carrapateena's underground operations and processing infrastructure. Initial EPCM services have commenced for early-phase activities.

Worley will provide full delivery and technical expertise through its team in Australia, supported by industry-leading specialist engineering service providers and Worley's Globally Integrated Delivery team, as well as support from China and Chile. The scope spans underground mining, processing and infrastructure across a connected "cave to concentrate" program.

Worley's consulting teams in APAC have helped inform BHP's growth plans through a range of projects over the last two years, including development option studies, techno-economic modelling, logistics assessments and support for project planning activities.

"This contract reflects Worley's continued focus on energy transition materials, such as copper, as a priority growth market," said Chris Ashton, Chief Executive Officer of Worley. "We are pleased to provide ongoing support for BHP's ambition to grow its copper production in South Australia."

He added: "Our team brings experience in underground mining, processing and major project delivery, and we look forward to working with BHP and the local businesses in South Australia to progress these mega projects."

Pedram Ittehadi, Head of CuSA Major Projects, BHP: "We are proud to be partnering with Worley to help deliver the next phase of Copper South Australia's growth. These projects are focused on improving capital intensity and unlocking additional value from our world-class assets through targeted expansions of material handling and concentrating facilities at both Carrapateena and Olympic Dam."

He added: "Worley's capability and experience will be important as we progress our plans to sustainably grow copper production and meet increasing global demand for this critical commodity."

"Delivering projects of this scale requires access to the very best expertise and delivery capability. We look forward to our partnership with Worley as they leverage their global scale and specialist capabilities in delivering large, complex programs in order to underpin our long-term growth ambitions in South Australia," said Nathan McGuire, Vice President Projects Commercial Minerals Australia, BHP.

This award Worley says reinforces its focus on full project delivery, bringing together studies, engineering and EPCM to support customers across the life of their assets, and builds on its experience supporting copper projects globally.
