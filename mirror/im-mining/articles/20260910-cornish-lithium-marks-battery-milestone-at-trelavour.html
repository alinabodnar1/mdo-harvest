Cornish Lithium marks battery milestone at Trelavour lithium project

Cornish Lithium has announced that lithium hydroxide monohydrate (LHM) produced from ore mined at its Trelavour lithium project and processed at the company's demonstration plant has been successfully used to manufacture coin cell batteries.

This milestone, the company says, represents an important step towards establishing a secure domestic supply of battery-grade lithium for the UK.

Cornish Lithium supplied approximately two kilograms of battery-grade LHM using its proprietary LiStract™ technology to the translational research and innovation organisation CPI for independent battery cell manufacture and testing.

Using the material provided by Cornish Lithium, CPI produced and evaluated lithium-ion coin cell batteries to assess electrochemical performance. The batteries performed in line with and, in some areas, exceeded the performance of comparable cells manufactured using commercially available LHM, it reported.

The successful test program provides independent validation of both the battery-grade quality of the LHM produced at Trelavour and the effectiveness of Cornish Lithium's innovative LiStract process, according to Cornish Lithium.

Jamie Airnes, CEO of Cornish Lithium, said: "Our team at Trelavour has undertaken a significant amount of work developing and optimising our demonstration plant to produce a high-quality lithium hydroxide that meets the demanding specifications of the battery industry. The successful battery testing undertaken with CPI provides valuable real-world validation of both our lithium hydroxide product and our proprietary technology. This places Cornish Lithium in a strong position as we continue to advance the development of our Trelavour lithium project and engage with potential customers and offtake partners. This work also reinforces our decision to invest in the demonstration plant and validates the effectiveness of a process that has been extensively tested and refined over several years. The technology enables us to produce battery-grade lithium hydroxide from domestic resources and supports our ambition to establish a secure and sustainable lithium supply chain to support the UK's growing battery manufacturing sector."
