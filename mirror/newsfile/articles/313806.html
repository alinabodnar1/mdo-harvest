Amex découvre une nouvelle zone aurifère, Rosé Zone, environ 165 m sous sa rampe entièrement financée en développement

Montréal, Québec--(Newsfile Corp. - 10 septembre 2026) - Les Mines d'Or Amex Inc. (TSXV: AMX) (FSE: MX0) (OTCQX: AMXEF) is pleased to announce the discovery of a new gold-bearing zone, on the Perron project in the Abitibi region of Quebec. The new zone, "Rosé Zone", is located approximately 165 m directly below the ramp infrastructure currently under development as part of the bulk sampling program. The Rosé Zone allows Amex to continue adding significant additional ounces to the project. The zone has been minimally drilled and one of its deepest holes, PE-26-950 at 410 m, contains visible gold "VG", suggesting the zone opens at depth.

Additionally, Amex is pleased to present an update on exploration programs on the Perron and Perron West properties. Drilling coordinates are presented in Tables 5 and 6.

Rosé Zone Drilling Highlights (Table 1):

8.02 g/t Au over 4.30 m, including 23.93 g/t Au (and 8.80 g/t Ag) over 1.15 m at approximately 210 m vertical depth in hole PE-26-934

11.78 g/t Au over 1.80 m including 30.86 g/t Au (and 4.70 g/t Ag) over 0.65 m at approximately 290 m vertical depth in hole PE-26-908

10.94 g/t Au over 1.20 m including 21.22 g/t Au (and 4.30 g/t Ag) over 0.55 m at approximately 280 m vertical depth in hole PE-25-878

The discovery of the Rosé Zone near planned underground infrastructure is beneficial to Amex for several reasons:

1. Cost savings on exploration - There is potential to drill extensions of the new zone from new underground developments,

2. The new zone is located near ongoing underground development work, which would limit the underground development required to access it in the future mining plan.

Readers are advised that no economic assumptions can be made regarding newly encountered mineralization until an updated mineral resource estimate and accompanying updated project economic evaluation have been completed.

"Our 2026 drilling program has been successful to date on several fronts," says Aaron Stone, Vice-President Exploration at Amex Gold Mining. "What really distinguishes the Rosé Zone is its discovery near our ramp under development and already fully funded. Although still in early exploration stages, the Rosé Zone has the potential to add significant ounces to proposed future operations. Initial team observations suggest mineralization is hosted in a set of tension veins from a larger structure, which could also control mineralization of the Alizée zone to the north-northwest. Further drilling and modeling work are planned to validate this hypothesis."

Zone interpretation is ongoing, but preliminary observations draw similarities to the Alizée Zone to the north, where the discovery hole returned a gold intersection at 31.87 g/t Au over 5.10 m (see release dated January 25, 2026). For now, the new zone has been defined over a vertical extension of 275 m and a strike length of 70 m, and remains open in several directions.

Drilling at the Perron West Property

Figure 8 presents an overview of Amex's first drilling program at the Perron West property in Ontario. To date, drilling has been completed in target zone D, with others underway in zones A and C. Zone B remains undrilled. A total of 7675 core sample analyses (including 1654 soil sample analyses) are pending results from Amex's partner laboratories.

Figure 8: Amex's initial drilling program at the Perron West property, Ontario.

For an enhanced version of this graphic, please visit: https://images.newsfilecorp.com/files/2667/313806_e8043b3b04ca6aea_009full.jpg

The Amex team is very encouraged by preliminary observations on this drilling program. Most holes have intercepted significant sulfide mineralizations and/or large quartz veins with alteration assemblages similar to those observed on the Perron project in Quebec.

In sector A, hole PW-26-002, the second hole drilled on the property, intercepted a prospective sulfide horizon whose laboratory analysis returned a result of 22.20 g/t Ag, 0.295 Cu and 0.34% Zn over 1.20 m (see Figures 9A and 10, Table 4). Given the close relationship between SMV and gold mineralizations on the Perron property in Quebec, the Amex team is very encouraged by both the prospect of a new SMV-type discovery and also the vectoring opportunity toward potential gold zones in the sector.

In sector D (Figures 9B and 11, Table 4), multiple zones with sulfide mineralization were intercepted as well as anomalous gold values in holes PW-26-010 and PW-26-012. These results potentially suggest the emergence of a new gold zone. With approximately 1 km between the two anomalous grades, Amex's geology team has begun work to better understand potential controls on mineralization in this sector. Observed alteration assemblages contain quartz, sericite, carbonate and chlorite and share geological similarities to known resources on Perron in Quebec.

Several holes also intercepted large multi-meter quartz veins, many of which laboratory analysis results are still pending. Hole PW-26-001, the first hole completed on the property, intercepted a large milky to smoky quartz vein 1.8 m in length, with notable chlorite alteration. Chlorite alteration is often observed in veins of the Champagne Zone. Hole PW-26-011, which is in the lateral periphery of the SMV mineralization of hole PW-26-002, intercepted not only significant sulfide mineralizations but large and multiple smoky quartz veins (Figure 10). The Company is encouraged by these observations and plans follow-up drilling around this possible mineralized axis, which appears to correspond to a weak electromagnetic "EM" signature. Amex plans downhole electromagnetic surveys "BHEM" in several holes on the Perron West property to measure this EM signature more precisely and better model this potential mineralized axis.

About Amex

Amex Exploration Inc. has made significant high-grade gold discoveries, as well as copper-rich volcanogenic massive sulfide (VMS) zones, as part of its 100%-owned Perron gold project, located approximately 110 kilometers north of Rouyn-Noranda, Quebec. The Perron project consists of 183 contiguous claims, covering an area of 65.75 km². The project hosts both high-tonnage and high-grade gold mineralizations.

Combined with the adjacent and contiguous Perron West, Abbotsford and Hepburn projects (including additional claims acquired by staking) in Ontario, the consolidated land portfolio covers an area of 501.08 km². This vast property is located in a highly promising geological zone, conducive to high-grade gold mineralization and VMS mineralization.

The project benefits from excellent infrastructure: accessible by a year-round road, it is located only 30 minutes from an airport and approximately 6.5 km from the town of Normétal. It is also located near several processing plants owned by major gold producers.

Qualified Persons and QA&QC

The geological information in this release has been reviewed and approved by Aaron Stone, P. Geo (OGQ - 2170, PGO - 3708), Vice-President Exploration of the Company as well as by Jérôme Augustin P.Geo. Ph.D., (OGQ 2134), an independent qualified person within the meaning of Regulation 43-101.

The execution of the drilling campaign, quality assurance and quality control protocols were planned and supervised by Jérôme Augustin. Core description and sampling were performed by Laurentia Exploration's geology team.

Quality assurance and quality control protocols include the insertion of blank or standard samples every 10 samples on average, in addition to the regular insertion of blank, duplicate and standard samples certified by Expert Laboratory and AGAT Laboratories Ltd during the analytical process.

For all analyses targeting gold mineralization, gold grades are determined by fire assay with atomic absorption finish. Values exceeding 3 ppm gold are reanalyzed by fire assay with gravimetric finish by Expert Laboratory Inc., Rouyn-Noranda. Samples containing visible gold are analyzed by screen metallic analysis. For additional quality assurance and control purposes, samples were ground to 90% to a fraction smaller than 2 mm before being pulverized, to homogenize samples that may contain coarse gold grain.

For all analyses targeting gold and base metal mineralization, gold, platinum and palladium grades were determined by fire assay with atomic absorption finish. Grades in other metals and trace elements are determined by four-acid digestion and ICP-EOS/MS finish, by AGAT Laboratories. For additional quality assurance and control purposes, samples were ground to a fraction smaller than 2 mm and then to 85% passing a 75 micron screen, to homogenize samples that may contain coarse gold grain.
